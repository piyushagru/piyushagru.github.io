# 01 — Approved portfolio brief

## Purpose
Create a personal portfolio for Piyush Aggarwal, a software engineer building customer-facing and platform systems at Amazon. Communicate technical depth, ownership, product impact, and a human personality through a distinctive cinematic visual story.

This is not a generic SaaS landing page, template portfolio, resume pasted into cards, or AI-generated component gallery.

## Approved concept
A single continuous-scroll portfolio with a warm cinematic landscape as its visual thread: distant mountain ranges, closer ridgelines, drifting clouds, atmospheric mist, sunset light, restrained foliage, and a small sparrow as a recurring character and interaction device.

Current visual source: the Portfolio Design System v3 board and the sunset mountain hero reference in raw/design-references/. Preserve their composition and identity. Improve quality and execution; do not replace the concept.

## Intended narrative / sections
1. Hero / Introduction — personal introduction, clear engineering positioning, scenic mountain environment, compact navigation, explore-work CTA, contact CTA.
2. Engineering Philosophy — how Piyush thinks about building software, expressed with specific ideas such as distributed systems, scale/reliability, and ownership.
3. Selected Work — Ads in Amazon Now, Speed in Search, Lifetime Savings Engine. Custom diagrams explain the actual systems.
4. Experience — Amazon Fresh (SDE I) followed by Amazon Now (SDE II), with real dates and meaningful scope.
5. Beyond the Systems / Closing — personal end scene, verified personal interests, contact/social links, and the sparrow returning to the final scene.

The v3 board has an AI & Experiments panel between Work and Experience. That was a proposal in an earlier board and is superseded by the user's later instruction to remove generic/placeholder AI content. Do not add it back unless Piyush supplies real work and explicitly asks for it.

## Navigation
Use compact anchor navigation for the same page. Suggested items: About, Engineering, Work, Experience, Beyond, plus a Let's Connect CTA. Remove the AI item shown in the older board.

## Tone
Confident, thoughtful, human, specific. Avoid empty filler such as “passionate about technology”, “pushing boundaries”, or “innovating the future”.

## Premium quality bar
- Cohesive custom art direction from top to bottom.
- High-quality landscape artwork and bespoke technical diagrams.
- Excellent typography, spacing, hierarchy, responsive composition.
- Motion supports the story and does not compete with reading.
- Every major section feels like a deliberate viewport-sized chapter.
- Desktop and mobile both designed intentionally.
