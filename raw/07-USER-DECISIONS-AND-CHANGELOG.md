This preserves the important user requirements in the language used during the design discussion. Later direct instructions take precedence over earlier mockup proposals.

## Direct user requests

### Design sheets and responsive layouts

> “where is motion graphic and can you help me with desktop and mobile design style sheet per page? and also including opening and closing”

### Clouds and sparrow pointer

> “you can have clouds in first screen as continously moving horizontly”

> “mouse can become a small sparrow bird in that page or in that area”

> “flapping it wings as we move the cursor”

### Quality correction and sequential illustrations

> “remove any and all AI slop also parrot is very poor i hope we will see quality in actual site and left right smooth movement of the bird content should also load one by one as in illustations should load first first the mountaitns range that are in front then in second layer in back then third in backa nd then more back and so on”

Interpretation: the bird must be a recognisable sparrow, not a parrot; it should move smoothly left/right with the pointer; scenic layers appear sequentially with the nearest foreground mountain first and increasingly distant layers behind; content reveals one at a time after the illustrations.

### Viewport-sized sections and ending sparrow

> “add sparrow again in the last page ending one”

> “also every page should occupy window size of the device so that it feel more premium”

Interpretation: bring the sparrow back in the closing scene; each major section should feel like a device-viewport chapter. Use minimum height rather than fixed height so content is not clipped.

### Preserve approved template and elevate it

> “yes now raw template is good i want you to scale this to a very very nice premium look without having any AI Slop”

Interpretation: improve the approved visual direction and quality; do not replace the template with an unrelated design.

### Raw handoff

> “i would need you to also dump all approved raw things and requriement and design sheet and stuff that we finalised in a raw folder for me to take up with another ai tool”

### Correction after wholesale redesign

> “you have changed whole site... why?”

Interpretation: do not wholesale-rewrite the portfolio. Preserve the approved identity, page narrative, and visual design. Work incrementally, component by component, and get review before broad structural changes.

## Approval hierarchy
1. Direct user instructions in this conversation are highest priority.
2. The warm cinematic v3 design-system sheet and sunset mountain hero are the current visual references.
3. Earlier sheets are retained for history and comparison; they do not override later corrections.
4. Mock text/labels in generated boards are not verified facts. Use 04-CONTENT-AND-FACTS.md for actual content.

## Not approved / superseded
- Generic AI/Experiments section or invented AI project claims.
- Low-quality parrot-like bird, rough bird SVG, emoji bird, or generic pointer icon.
- Replacing the whole site with a different layout/style because a redesign seems more modern.
- Reversing the mountain reveal sequence (foreground appears first).
- Fixed-height sections that cut off text or content.
