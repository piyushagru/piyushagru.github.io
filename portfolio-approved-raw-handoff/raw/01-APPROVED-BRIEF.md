# 01 — Approved portfolio brief

## Purpose

Create a personal portfolio for Piyush Aggarwal, a software engineer building customer-facing and platform systems at Amazon. The portfolio should communicate technical depth, ownership, product impact, and a human personality through a distinctive cinematic visual story.

This is **not** a generic SaaS landing page, a template portfolio, a resume pasted into cards, or an AI-generated component gallery.

## Approved concept

A single, continuous-scroll portfolio with a warm, cinematic landscape as its visual thread. The landscape is layered and illustrated: distant mountain ranges, closer ridgelines, drifting clouds, atmospheric mist, a sunset/sunrise light source, and restrained foliage. The little sparrow is a recurring character and interaction device.

The latest approved design direction is shown in:

- `design-references/portfolio-design-system-v3.webp`
- `design-references/sunset-mountain-hero-reference.webp`

The v3 sheet shows a desktop long-page composition, mobile responsive version, motion/interaction system, opening sequence, closing sequence, and a storyboard. Keep its overall composition and identity. Improve craft and execution; do not replace the concept.

## Intended narrative / sections

Keep the continuous-scroll narrative in this order:

1. **Hero / Introduction** — personal introduction, clear engineering positioning, the scenic mountain environment, navigation, primary action to explore work, and contact action.
2. **Engineering Philosophy** — how Piyush thinks about building software, expressed with clear points such as distributed systems, scale/reliability, and ownership. Keep the copy specific and grounded.
3. **Selected Work** — the three verified projects: Ads in Amazon Now, Speed in Search, and Lifetime Savings Engine. The work should be the centrepiece, not generic project cards. Use custom diagrams/illustrations to explain the actual systems.
4. **Experience** — career timeline showing Amazon Fresh (SDE I) followed by Amazon Now (SDE II), with real dates and meaningful scope.
5. **Beyond the Systems / Closing** — a personal end scene with the landscape returning, personal interests that are actually known, social/contact links, and the sparrow returning to the final scene.

The v3 sheet has an `AI & Experiments` section between work and experience. That was a proposal in an earlier board and is **superseded** by the user's later instruction to remove generic/placeholder AI content. Do not add it back as a page or nav item unless Piyush later supplies real work and explicitly asks for it.

## Navigation

Keep a compact top navigation that anchors into the same page. The v3 mockup shows About, Engineering, Work, AI, Experience, Beyond and a “Let’s Connect” CTA; remove the AI item. A reasonable final set is About, Engineering, Work, Experience, Beyond, plus Let’s Connect. Avoid inventing extra pages or navigation systems.

## Tone and content

The writing should feel confident, thoughtful, and human. Prefer short, specific statements over inflated claims. Use Piyush's actual experience and outcomes. Do not use generic filler such as “passionate about technology”, “pushing boundaries”, “innovating the future”, or other empty portfolio clichés.

## Premium quality bar

- A coherent, custom art direction across the entire scroll.
- Detailed landscape artwork and custom diagrams, with consistent line weight, shape language, and lighting.
- Excellent typography, spacing, hierarchy, and responsive composition.
- Motion should support the story and demonstrate craft, not compete with reading.
- Every major section should feel like a deliberate viewport-sized chapter.
- Desktop and mobile must both be designed intentionally, not just shrink the desktop view.
